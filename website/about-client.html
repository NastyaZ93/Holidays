About our client

A large US online marketplace

Our client is the marketing team of a large US online marketplace. Every year they plan holiday campaigns and stock, and every year the same two questions come back: what should we promote, and when should we start?

The business question

Which product categories gain most from the holiday season, and how early does holiday shopping start?

What we looked at

📍 Where

The United States

🗓️ When

Six full holiday seasons, 2020 to 2025 (2026 is left out because the season is not finished)

📊 What

Monthly retail sales by category and weekly Google searches from September to December

Our three hypotheses

  1. November and December are the biggest spending peak of the year
  2. Gift categories drive the peak, not everyday shopping
  3. People start searching weeks before they buy
